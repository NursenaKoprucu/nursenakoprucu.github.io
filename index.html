Research

Matryoshka Gaussian Splatting

Zhilin Guo, Boqiao Zhang, Hakan Aktas, Kyle Fogarty, Jeffrey Hu, Nursena Koprucu Aslan, Wenzhao Li, Canberk Baykal, Albert Miao, Josef Bengtson, Chenliang Zhou, Weihao Xia, Cristina Nader Vasconcelos, Cengiz Öztireli

ECCV 2026

A standard Gaussian splatting model has one fixed size. We train a single ordered set of Gaussians so that the first k of them already form a coherent scene. One model then renders at any budget, and at full size it matches the quality of its backbone.

Telescopic Language Models

Zhilin Guo, Boqiao Zhang, Hakan Aktas, Kyle Fogarty, Nursena Koprucu Aslan, Wenzhao Li, Canberk Baykal, Albert Miao, Siyu Hong, Yixiao Liu, Adam Wu, Ashish Kumar Singh, Sakar Khattar, Chenliang Zhou, Weihao Xia, Cristina Nader Vasconcelos, Cengiz Öztireli

arXiv preprint, September 2026

A Transformer in which every depth prefix is a valid language model. Each training step supervises one randomly truncated prefix and the full model, with no change to the architecture. On a 200M-parameter suite trained on 20B tokens, one run is usable at all twenty depths.

NIMBA: Towards Robust and Principled Processing of Point Clouds With SSMs

Nursena Köprücü, Destiny Okpekpe, Antonio Orvieto

arXiv preprint, 2024. Work done at the Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems.

State-space models such as Mamba read a sequence in order, but a point cloud has no order. We convert a point cloud into a 1D sequence that keeps its 3D spatial structure. The model then needs no positional embeddings and shorter sequences, and it outperforms Transformer-based models in accuracy and efficiency on ModelNet40 and ScanObjectNN.
